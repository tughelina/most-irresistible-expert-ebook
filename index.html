The writing process

The chapters come last.

The AI interviews you through purposeful milestones, researches your audience, and helps write the transformation itself. Only then do you name and group the finished material into no more than five focused chapters, each with a search-relevant title and an objection-aware subtitle.

  1. Uncover your expertise and ideal reader
  2. Research, compare and choose one meaningful problem
  3. Define your transformative thesis and unique mechanism
  4. Bring in real stories and appropriate offers
  5. Develop the actual reader transformation
  6. Write and refine the manuscript together
  7. Reverse-engineer up to five keyword-relevant chapters
  8. Add an optional foreword and afterword, then audit and publish
