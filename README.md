# ✨ The Most Irresistible Expert Ebook

### Your expertise. Your voice. A focused ebook, developed with AI.

**Version:** 1.2.1 • **Authorial framework:** Chantal / House of Coaching • **Status:** ready for a supervised pilot, not yet validated with real customer sessions.

Turn your own expertise into a short, reader-focused ebook through a guided AI interview and writing process. Start with audience research, develop your actual method and stories, then shape the manuscript into up to five chapters.

**[📥 Download the free skill · v1.2.1](https://tughelina.github.io/most-irresistible-expert-ebook/most-irresistible-expert-ebook-v1.2.1.zip)** · **[🌐 Visit the landing page](https://tughelina.github.io/most-irresistible-expert-ebook/)**

## 💎 What you get

- **🔎 A relevant starting point:** research audience needs before choosing the one problem your ebook will address.
- **🗣️ Your own voice and method:** build the manuscript from your real expertise, writing samples and experiences.
- **✍️ Guided co-writing:** focused questions, manageable drafts and approval milestones throughout the process.
- **📖 A focused book:** one central problem, one meaningful reader result and no more than five chapters.
- **🧭 Practical value for readers:** reflection questions, exercises and decision tools that help them apply the ideas.
- **🤝 A natural next step:** connect your method to an invitation you choose and approve, such as a clarity conversation.
- **🎁 Free use for your own and client ebooks:** including commercial ebooks, under the [individual license](LICENSE).

## ✨ What makes this different from a fill-in ebook template?

| Part of the process | How this skill works |
| --- | --- |
| **The starting point** | Research comes first. Compare up to three audience problems and approve one before drafting. |
| **The chapter structure** | Develop the manuscript first, then group it into up to five chapters that fit the actual reader journey. |
| **The content** | Your real method, voice and stories shape the writing. Missing facts and sources are flagged for review. |
| **Titles and subtitles** | Each chapter gets a reader-relevant title and a subtitle addressing a real objection. |
| **Your control** | You approve key milestones, the manuscript and the final invitation. |

The download contains **AI skill instructions, reference guides, a project-state template and acceptance checks**. It guides content development; it does not supply a finished ebook or a visual book-layout template. Optional foreword and afterword sit outside the chapter limit, including a transparently credited AI-collaborator afterword.

## 🚀 Installation and activation

In AI environments supporting filesystem skills: place this folder in the app's skills directory as `most-irresistible-expert-ebook` and invoke/activate the skill by its name; check the app-specific skill instructions. There is **no universal import flow for every AI**. In ordinary chat systems that accept files, supply `SKILL.md` plus all reference files (or paste them), then say: "Activate The Most Irresistible Expert Ebook skill and begin at Milestone 1. Do not invent research or ask me to predefine chapter headings." Some systems cannot persist state automatically; save the project-state template externally between sessions.

In Claude Code, a conventional installation is to place the skill folder under `~/.claude/skills/most-irresistible-expert-ebook/` and invoke `/most-irresistible-expert-ebook` where supported. For other environments, use the platform’s current documentation; upload `SKILL.md` with `references/` and `templates/` as knowledge/instructions where supported, then give the activation sentence. Platform capabilities and import options change. `index.html` and this README are for people, not required model context.

## 🔍 Limitations

The skill **cannot itself grant internet access**, access private customer records, confirm payments, or guarantee search/AI recommendations. The user's AI must have a browsing/research capability, and original business claims need genuine author data. No customer story, empirical claim or book result should be published without review. Source handout is an editorial methodology, not a validation study.
